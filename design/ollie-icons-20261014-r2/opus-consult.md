## 1. What went wrong in round 1

- **Only the finish changed.** Teal glass, bone ceramic, chartreuse and silver keycap were material swatches on one idea. The user saw one concept four times.
- **A bolt on a squircle is a cliché.** Power tools, automation apps and battery utilities all use it. It says "fast" but tells you nothing about Ollie.
- **The menu bar and Dock icons have different jobs.** A menu bar template glyph should fade into the bar. A Dock icon needs to be an object or character you can spot among 40 others at a glance. Keeping the bolt was right for the menu bar but limited the Dock icon.
- **Flat SVG gave little depth or material.** Current macOS icons rely on lighting and texture. The next round needs ImageGen's tactility.

## 2. Directions

**Shared prompt suffix (append to every prompt):**
*"Square 1024×1024 macOS app icon: a single Apple-style rounded-square tile, centered with standard icon margin, frontal view, soft drop shadow beneath, plain light outer canvas. No text, no letters, no logos, no mockup, no scene, no labels."*

### A. Pocket Otter (character)
- **Form and meaning:** A sea otter peeks over the bottom edge of the tile, holding a smooth pebble to its chest. Real sea otters keep a favourite stone in a skin pocket under their arm. That is a clipboard: Ollie keeps your things close and hands them back. The name gets a face, and the face looks calm and capable.
- **Style:** A matte vinyl toy with sculpted planes and no fur strands. Chestnut and cream against deep ink-teal.
- **Fit:** Warm, ownable and easy to remember. Nothing else in the Dock looks like it.
- **Risk:** It could slide into a children's game. Keep the expression calm with no grin, and make the cream muzzle the main contrast so it still reads at 32px.
- **Prompt:** *"Friendly sea otter head and front paws peeking up over the bottom edge of a deep ink-teal (#12343B) tile, cradling a small smooth pale-grey pebble against its chest. Soft-touch matte vinyl-toy render, chunky simplified sculpted forms, no fur strands, wide-set glossy dark eyes, large cream (#F1E4CF) muzzle, warm chestnut (#8A5532) body. Calm, attentive expression. Gentle top-left key light, subtle cool rim light. Otter fills ~70% of the tile, face in the upper middle."*

### B. Selector Knob (physical control)
- **Form and meaning:** One chunky rotary selector knob with six detent ticks. One control switches between modes: launcher, clipboard, dictation and the rest. It feels like hardware made for people who work from the keyboard.
- **Style:** Braun or Teenage Engineering industrial design. Off-white knurled knob on bead-blasted warm grey aluminium, with a signal-orange pointer.
- **Fit:** Tactile, precise and compact. The silhouette (circle, ticks and pointer) survives at 32px.
- **Risk:** It could read as a volume or audio app. The tick marks are what say "modes", so never drop them.
- **Prompt:** *"Single chunky rotary selector knob seen straight from above, set into a warm grey bead-blasted aluminium tile (#B9B4AA). Off-white matte knob (#EEEAE2) with finely knurled edge and a recessed signal-orange (#FF5A1F) pointer line aimed at one o'clock. Six small engraved tick marks around the knob. Dieter Rams industrial product render, soft overhead studio light, crisp contact shadow, precise machined detail."*

### C. Smoke Portal (sculptural O)
- **Form and meaning:** A thick slab of smoked glass with an off-centre hole bored through it. Ember light glows only inside the hole. It is the "O" in Ollie and a doorway to everything else, and it carries over Tabbie's smoky glass.
- **Style:** Dark charcoal-teal glass, frosted surface, polished inner walls, amber light refracting around the inner rim.
- **Fit:** The most continuous with the current UI. Moody and premium.
- **Risk:** Ring icons are common. The off-centre hole and glow confined to the inside are what make it ownable. A centred, evenly lit ring kills the idea.
- **Prompt:** *"Thick slab of dark smoked charcoal-teal glass forming the tile, with a large circular hole bored clean through it, placed slightly lower-left of center. Polished inner bore walls catch a warm ember-amber (#FF9B3D) glow that refracts along the inner rim; the rest of the surface is frosted and smoky with faint internal haze and crisp beveled edges. Moody dark product-photography lighting, light only inside the ring."*

### D. Ollie Deck (wit / name)
- **Form and meaning:** A skateboard caught mid-ollie, nose popped up on a diagonal. An ollie is the basic jump, a quick hop over obstacles, which is what the app does between windows, clips and commands. The pun is the brand.
- **Style:** A two-colour screenprint with slight ink texture. Sky blue, black grip tape, maple edge, cream wheels.
- **Fit:** Playful and memorable, and the strong diagonal stands out among blocky Dock icons.
- **Risk:** It could be mistaken for a skate shop or game, and the joke may wear thin for people who don't skate.
- **Prompt:** *"Side view of a skateboard mid-ollie, tail down and nose popped up at a 35° diagonal, rising across a sky-blue (#6EC3F4) tile. Black grip-tape top, natural maple (#E3B77C) wood edge, two cream urethane wheels, one bold curved motion sweep beneath the tail. Flat two-color screenprint style with subtle ink grain, thick confident shapes, high contrast."*

### E. Folded Bolt (the one bolt descendant)
- **Form and meaning:** A single strip of thick red card folded twice into a zigzag. The bolt is no longer drawn; it is made from one continuous strip. It links back to the menu bar mark through a new medium and a new idea: one workflow with folds where you switch.
- **Style:** Paper-craft studio photograph. Tomato-red card on cream, visible creases, real shadows.
- **Fit:** Keeps the family tie to the menu bar while looking clearly handmade.
- **Risk:** If the folds are subtle, this becomes round 1 in paper. The folds and their shading must carry the image.
- **Prompt:** *"One long strip of thick tomato-red (#E8432E) card folded twice into a zigzag lightning shape, standing slightly off a cream (#F4EEE3) paper tile and casting soft real shadows. Crisp visible fold creases with each folded face a different shade, faint paper fiber texture. Tactile paper-craft studio photograph, soft window light from top-left."*

### F. Shift Cut (abstract slice)
- **Form and meaning:** A raised slab sliced diagonally, with the upper piece shifted down one precise step to reveal the layer beneath. It suggests the Shift key, moving windows and pasting with an offset.
- **Style:** Thick cut acrylic in International Klein Blue over cream. One hard shadow, nothing else.
- **Fit:** The purest graphic option and the easiest to read at 32px.
- **Risk:** It could look like a fintech or developer-tool logo, and it has the least warmth.
- **Prompt:** *"Raised slab of solid International Klein Blue (#1F2EC4) acrylic on a cream (#F2EBDD) tile base, sliced cleanly on a diagonal from upper-right to lower-left; the upper piece is offset down-right by one precise step, exposing a sliver of cream along the cut. Flat frontal view, minimal, one hard cast shadow along the stepped edge, crisp machined edges."*

## 3. Ranking

1. **Pocket Otter.** It changes silhouette, medium, palette and meaning all at once. The otter's stone pocket is a real reason to use an otter rather than mascot decoration, and it is the only option users would remember by name ("the otter app").
2. **Selector Knob.** Different in every way from round 1, and it suits a compact, keyboard-first, multi-mode tool better than any metaphor here. It also looks the most grown-up.

**The real break from round 1 is Pocket Otter**, with Ollie Deck close behind. Folded Bolt is the bridge back to the menu bar mark, and Smoke Portal is the safe option that matches the current UI.
